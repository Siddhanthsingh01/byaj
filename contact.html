Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How do I get started with BYAJ?

Download the BYAJ app from Google Play Store, create an account, and follow the setup wizard to begin managing your loans and finances.

Is my data secure?

Yes! BYAJ uses enterprise-grade encryption and Firebase's secure infrastructure. For details, check our Data Safety page.

How do I delete my account?

Visit our Delete Account page and fill out the deletion request form. We'll process it within 30 days.

What payment methods do you accept?

BYAJ is a free app available on Google Play Store. In-app purchases (if available) accept all standard payment methods supported by Google Play.

How do I backup my data?

BYAJ provides built-in backup functionality. You can enable automatic backups or manually backup your data through the app settings.

Can I export my data?

Yes, you can export your financial data in various formats through the app's export feature. Check your app settings for export options.
