Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your account with Byaj, please read the following information carefully.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • Your account will be permanently deleted
  • All associated data will be removed from our servers
  • This action cannot be undone
  • Any ongoing services will be terminated

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account, please contact our support team with your request. We will process your deletion within 30 days.

Questions?

If you have any questions about the account deletion process, please reach out to us.
