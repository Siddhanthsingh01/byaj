Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your account with BYAJ, please read the following information carefully. This is a permanent action that cannot be undone.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • Your account will be permanently deleted from our systems
  • All associated personal data will be removed from our servers
  • This action cannot be undone
  • Any ongoing services or subscriptions will be terminated immediately
  • You will no longer have access to any stored data in your account

Data Deletion Process

Upon account deletion, we will:

  • Remove all personal information from our primary databases
  • Delete associated files and backups within 30 days
  • Retain only transaction records as required by law
  • Send you a confirmation email once deletion is complete

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account, please contact our support team with your request. Include:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your full name or username
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

We will process your deletion request within 30 days and send you a confirmation.

Before You Delete

Please ensure you have:

  • Downloaded or exported any data you wish to keep
  • Settled all outstanding balances
  • Updated any linked services or integrations

Questions?

If you have any questions about the account deletion process, please reach out to us. We're here to help.
