Data Safety & Security

Last updated: July 2026

Our Commitment to Data Security

At BYAJ, we take data security seriously and implement industry-standard measures to protect your financial information. Your trust is our priority.

Security Measures

  • End-to-end encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for account access
  • Secure authentication protocols (OAuth 2.0, JWT)
  • Limited access to personal information (principle of least privilege)
  • Regular employee training on data protection and security
  • Compliance with international security standards (ISO 27001)
  • Automatic session timeout and activity logging

Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure, encrypted servers with redundant backup systems. We use industry-leading cloud infrastructure with:

  • Geographically distributed data centers
  • Automatic daily backups
  • Disaster recovery protocols
  • 99.99% uptime guarantee

Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You can request deletion or export of your data at any time through your account settings or by contacting our support team.

Financial Data Protection

BYAJ is designed specifically for financial data management with enhanced security:

  • PCI DSS compliance for payment information
  • Real-time fraud detection and prevention
  • Secure transaction logging and audit trails
  • Encrypted financial records storage

Reporting a Security Issue

If you discover a security vulnerability or suspicious activity, please contact us immediately. We take all security concerns seriously and will address them promptly. Do not share vulnerability details publicly until we've had time to respond.

Privacy Controls

You have full control over your data:

  • View what data we collect about you
  • Export your data in standard formats
  • Delete specific data or your entire account
  • Control what information is shared with third parties
  • Opt-out of non-essential communications

Third-Party Security

We only partner with vetted third-party services that meet our security standards. All integrations are monitored and regularly audited for compliance.
